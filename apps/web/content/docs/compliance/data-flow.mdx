## In short

- The Zowork Forms builder holds forms. It doesn't receive or store respondents' answers. [TODO: legal review]
- `@zoworkhq/forms` sends answers from the respondent's browser to your endpoint, and makes no other request with them.
- Until the form is sent, the package may keep the answers in the browser, depending on the form's drafts mode.
- Once they reach your server, where they go is up to your app.

This page states what the software does. It doesn't say what that means under any law or agreement that applies to you. [TODO: legal review]

## What the builder holds

The builder's database has tables for:

- the authors' accounts, their organizations, members and invites;
- forms, and every saved version of each one;
- templates;
- a log of administrators' actions.

It has no table for answers, and the builder's API has no route that receives them. The form file an author downloads is the form itself: its questions, conditions, scores and settings, with no answers.

## The path of an answer

1. **Your app serves the page.** The form file and the package are part of your app. The form doesn't load either from Zowork.
2. **The respondent fills in the form.** Their answers are in the page's memory. Depending on the drafts mode, a copy is also kept in the tab, an encrypted draft on the device, or a draft on your server. See [What is stored in the browser](/docs/integrate/concepts/browser-storage).
3. **The respondent submits.** The form sends one `POST`, with the answers as JSON, to the `endpoint` you gave it. This is the only request the package makes with answers in it.
4. **Your server checks it.** `prepareSubmission` runs on your server and makes no requests of its own. You store `result.submission` wherever your app stores data.
5. **The form tidies up.** After your endpoint answers with a 2xx, the form deletes the tab's copy and any device draft, and calls your `deleteDraft` for a server draft.

**The endpoint must be `https://`,** or `http://` on `localhost` or `127.0.0.1`. The form refuses any other absolute address. A relative path uses the page's own protocol, so on a plain-HTTP page the answers are sent unencrypted.

**What is sent** is listed field by field in [The posted body](/docs/integrate/reference/posted-body): the answers to visible questions, an `idempotencyKey`, the respondent's clock time, and the form's ids. Answers to questions hidden by conditions are never in it.

## Requests without answers

A form's images, video and audio load from wherever the author pointed them, from the respondent's browser:

- **Image files** are requested with no referrer.
- **YouTube and Vimeo videos** play in those services' own players, in a frame: YouTube from `www.youtube-nocookie.com`, Vimeo with `dnt=1`. The players are sent your site's origin, never the page's path.
- **Video and audio files** on another host are requested with your page's referrer policy.

None of these requests carries answers. What those services and hosts do with the requests is outside the package. [Serve images, video and audio](/docs/integrate/media) has the details.

## What the package keeps in the browser

| Mode | Kept in the browser |
|---|---|
| `off` | Nothing |
| Only while the form is open | A copy of the visible answers in the tab's `sessionStorage`, as plain text |
| On this device | That copy, with answers to hidden questions too, and a draft in IndexedDB, encrypted with your key |
| On the app's server | That copy, with answers to hidden questions too. The draft itself is on your server. |

It sets no cookies and writes nothing to `localStorage`. [What is stored in the browser](/docs/integrate/concepts/browser-storage) has every key, how long each lasts, and what deletes it.

## What your app decides

The package and the builder leave these to you: [TODO: legal review]

- **Where submissions are stored,** who can read them and how long they're kept.
- **Server-mode drafts:** where they're stored, who can read them and how long they're kept. The package doesn't expire them.
- **The key for device-mode drafts.** The package never makes, stores or exports it.
- **Shared devices:** the drafts mode, and clearing saved progress between people. See [Shared devices and kiosks](/docs/integrate/shared-devices).
- **Logs.** Submission and draft bodies carry respondents' answers. Keep them out of request logs, error reports and analytics, and log ids such as `formId`, `formVersionId` and `idempotencyKey` instead.
- **Your page's headers,** such as its [Content Security Policy](/docs/integrate/content-security-policy) and referrer policy.

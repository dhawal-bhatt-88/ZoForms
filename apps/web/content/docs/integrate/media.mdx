## When you'd use this

<UseCases>
<UseCase title="Files on your own server">
A form's map, welcome video or audio directions live in your app, at a path such as `/images/map.png`.
</UseCase>
<UseCase title="An app under a sub-path">
Your app is served from `/clinic/`, so those paths need to load from under it.
</UseCase>
<UseCase title="Captions on another host">
A video's captions file is on a CDN, and needs CORS.
</UseCase>
</UseCases>

## Before you start

- A form with image, video or audio blocks. The author sets each block's source in the builder; see [Image](/docs/build/layout-and-media/image), [Video](/docs/build/layout-and-media/video) and [Audio](/docs/build/layout-and-media/audio).

## Steps

### 1. Serve the files the form names

A source is an `https://` URL, or a path on your own server starting with a single `/`, such as `/images/map.png`. Anything else isn't shown.

| Block | Accepted |
|---|---|
| Image | Any such source |
| Video | A YouTube or Vimeo link, shown in that service's player, or an `.mp4` or `.webm` file |
| Video captions | A `.vtt` file, for a video file only |
| Audio | An `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg` or `.wav` file |

A source that isn't accepted, or a file that fails to load, shows nothing: never a broken image or an error.

### 2. Pass `assetBase` if your app lives under a sub-path

Paths starting with `/` load from your page's origin. If your app is served under a sub-path, pass it as `assetBase`, so `/images/map.png` loads from `/clinic/images/map.png`. With Vite, pass `import.meta.env.BASE_URL`.

```jsx check=fragment
<FormRenderer schema={schema} endpoint="/api/submit" assetBase="/clinic/" />
```

`https://` sources ignore it.

### 3. Set CORS for captions on another host

A captions file on a different origin from your page only loads with CORS, so the renderer then requests the video with CORS too. Serve both the captions file and the video file with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`. If the video won't load that way, it's shown once more without captions, with a warning in development, rather than not at all.

### 4. Keep your page's referrer policy strict

The players are sent only your site's origin, never the page path, and images are requested with `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`. A `<video>` or `<audio>` element has no referrer setting of its own, though, so a file on another host is requested with your page's referrer policy. Keep `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, the browser's default, or stricter, so the page path is never sent.

### 5. Allow them in your Content Security Policy

Allow the two players in `frame-src`, and wherever your video, audio and captions files live in `media-src`. See [Content Security Policy](/docs/integrate/content-security-policy).

## What happens behind the scenes

- Videos and audio never play by themselves. Audio never loops or starts muted.
- Captions on a video file are off until the respondent turns them on in the player.
- YouTube loads with `rel=0`, which only limits the suggestions at the end of a video to the same channel. It doesn't remove them.
- Captions on YouTube and Vimeo are set on that service, by the author.
- Recorded audio needs a text alternative to meet WCAG 2.1 AA (1.2.1). That's the author's to write, in a text block next to the clip.

## Troubleshooting

**An image or video shows nothing.** Its source isn't accepted, such as an `http://` URL, a relative path without the leading `/`, or a video on another service. Or the file failed to load: check the browser's network panel.

**Files load from the wrong place under a sub-path.** Pass `assetBase`.

**A video plays without its captions.** The captions file is on another origin without `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`, isn't a `.vtt` file, or failed to load.

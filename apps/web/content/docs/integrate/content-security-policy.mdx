## Before you start

- A page that renders `FormRenderer`, and a way to send headers from your app.

## Steps

### 1. Start from this policy

The package needs no inline scripts or styles and doesn't use `eval`, so a strict policy works. This one covers everything the package can do, served from your own origin:

```text
default-src 'self';
script-src 'self';
style-src 'self';
img-src 'self' https:;
media-src 'self';
connect-src 'self';
frame-src https://www.youtube-nocookie.com https://player.vimeo.com;
object-src 'none';
base-uri 'none';
```

It also works with `require-trusted-types-for 'script'`.

### 2. Narrow it to your forms

| Directive | Why | When to change it |
|---|---|---|
| `style-src` | The stylesheet, as a file | Add your stylesheet's origin if it's elsewhere. |
| `img-src` | Image blocks. `https:` allows the `https://` sources authors may use. | Narrow it to your hosts if you can. |
| `media-src` | Video and audio files, and captions | Add the hosts they're served from. |
| `connect-src` | The `POST` to `endpoint` | Add the endpoint's origin if it's on another one. |
| `frame-src` | YouTube and Vimeo video blocks | Drop it if your forms have none. |

### 3. Add what Next.js needs

Next.js adds inline scripts of its own to every page. [Its guide to Content Security Policy](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/content-security-policy) explains the two ways to allow them under a strict `script-src`: a nonce made for each request in Proxy, which needs every page rendered dynamically, or its experimental Subresource Integrity option. Follow that guide for Next.js's part.

The package needs neither: it adds no inline scripts. Its one inline style is the progress bar's on a paged form rendered on the server, below, and a nonce doesn't cover a `style` attribute.

## What happens behind the scenes

- The form's only request of its own is the `POST` to `endpoint`, hence `connect-src`.
- Images are requested with `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`. YouTube and Vimeo players are sent only your site's origin, never the page path.
- **One exception.** A paged form rendered on the server has `style="display:none"` on its progress bar, which `style-src` without `'unsafe-inline'` blocks. The bar then shows, even if the author didn't ask for one, and it stays after hydration. It's only a visual difference, and a form rendered in the browser isn't affected.

## Troubleshooting

**Every submit fails, and the console reports a `connect-src` violation.** The endpoint is on another origin. Add it to `connect-src`.

**A video shows nothing.** Its player isn't in `frame-src`, or its file's host isn't in `media-src`.

**A paged form shows a progress bar the author didn't ask for.** That's the exception above.

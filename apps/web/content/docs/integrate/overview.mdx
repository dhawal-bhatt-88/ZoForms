## In short

An author builds a form in the Zowork Forms builder and downloads it as a JSON file. You put that file in your app, render it with `@zoworkhq/forms`, and receive the answers on your own server. The file is all your app needs from the builder.

## Who does what

| Part | Who runs it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Zowork Forms builder | Your form author | Holds the form's questions, logic and scores, and produces the form file. |
| Form file | You keep it in your app | JSON that describes one saved version of the form. Each file has a `formId` and a `formVersionId`. |
| `@zoworkhq/forms` in the browser | Your app | Shows the form, checks the answers, keeps the respondent's progress, and posts to your endpoint. |
| `@zoworkhq/forms` on your server | Your server | Checks the posted body against the same form file and gives you the submission to store. |
| Your endpoint and storage | You | Receive, check and store each submission. |

## What you write

- A page that renders `FormRenderer` with the form file and your endpoint.
- An endpoint that passes the posted body to `prepareSubmission`, answers with a 2xx, and stores `result.submission`.
- Your own storage. The package stores no submissions.

Everything else is optional: [your own validators, drafts, sign-in and styling](/docs/integrate/reference/form-renderer) are all props or CSS.

## The package's three entry points

| Import | For | Runs in |
|---|---|---|
| `@zoworkhq/forms/react` | `FormRenderer`, `clearDrafts` and `classNames` | The browser. ES modules only. |
| `@zoworkhq/forms/core` | `prepareSubmission` and the rest of the logic, with no React | Your server, or anywhere else. ES modules and CommonJS. |
| `@zoworkhq/forms/styles.css` | The default stylesheet, which is optional | The browser. |

There is no bare `@zoworkhq/forms` import. It needs `react` and `react-dom` 18 or 19, and on a server `/core` needs Node 20 or later.

## Where answers go

- The builder holds form definitions. It has nowhere to put answers: its database has no table for them.
- `FormRenderer` makes one kind of request of its own: the `POST` to the `endpoint` you give it. It doesn't call the builder.
- What a respondent types stays in their browser until that `POST`. How much of it is kept in the browser in the meantime depends on the form's drafts setting.
- Images, video and audio in a form load from wherever the author pointed them, and YouTube and Vimeo videos load in those services' players. Those requests come from the respondent's browser, and they carry no answers.
